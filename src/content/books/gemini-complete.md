Gemini reads your Gmail. It edits your Docs. It analyzes your Sheets. It builds your Slides. It summarizes your Meet calls and searches across your Drive. It generates images with Imagen, understands video natively, runs Deep Research with citations, and grounds its answers in real-time Google Search.

If your work already lives in Google Workspace, Gemini isn't an add-on — it's a force multiplier most users have barely touched.

*Gemini: The Complete Guide* is the comprehensive, up-to-date guide to everything Gemini can do in 2026 — written for real people, not developers, not AI researchers. Every feature, every plan, every practical use case, in plain English.

## What's inside

**Get started right.** Choose the right plan (Free, Google AI Plus, AI Pro, AI Ultra, or Workspace add-on), set up Gemini across web/Android/iOS/Chrome, understand the Gemini 2.5 Pro and Flash model family, and have your first productive conversations.

**Master the core skills.** Writing, research with Google Search grounding, everyday productivity, and professional work — with concrete walkthroughs you can follow along with.

**Unlock every feature.** Native image generation with Imagen 3. Native video and audio understanding. YouTube summarization. Live audio conversations. Canvas for collaborative content. Gems (custom assistants). Extensions for Maps, Flights, YouTube, and Workspace. Deep Research and NotebookLM integration. The 1M+ token context window.

**Live inside Google Workspace.** The chapter most other Gemini books skip — Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, and Drive, with workflows that pull context from your actual work.

**Get better results.** Prompting fundamentals tuned for Gemini, strategies for working with massive context windows, Deep Think for complex reasoning, and output formatting for any destination.

**Use it for your world.** Targeted chapters for students and educators, business teams, developers (Gemini Code Assist, Vertex AI), and creators (Imagen 3, video understanding).

**Stay safe and informed.** Honest coverage of hallucinations, image-generation biases, Google Activity controls, Workspace data protections, and the ethical questions every user should be thinking about.

**75+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category — writing, research, business, creative, education, productivity, and technical.

Whether you're opening Gemini for the first time or you've been using it daily and want to finally understand the full toolkit, this is the only book you need.

Includes coverage of Gemini 2.5 Pro, 2.5 Flash, Deep Think, Deep Research, Imagen 3, Gems, Canvas, Workspace integration, NotebookLM, and every 2026 feature.
