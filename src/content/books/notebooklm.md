NotebookLM looks deceptively simple: upload some sources, ask questions, get answers. But under that quiet interface is a tool that can turn a 200-page report into a 15-minute podcast, a stack of research papers into an interactive video, or a year's worth of meeting notes into a searchable, citation-grounded knowledge base.

Most users never get past the first feature.

This book changes that.

*NotebookLM: The Complete Guide* is the comprehensive, up-to-date guide to everything NotebookLM can do in 2026 — every Studio output, every source type, every tier. Written for real people doing real research, in plain English, with concrete walkthroughs.

## What's inside

**Get started right.** Choose the right plan (Free, Plus, Pro, or Ultra), understand how source-grounded AI differs from ChatGPT, set up your account, and build your first useful notebook.

**Master the sources.** PDFs, Google Docs, websites, YouTube, audio, video, images with OCR — every source type, with strategy for which combinations actually produce good answers.

**Unlock the Studio.** Audio Overviews, Video Overviews, Cinematic Mode, Mind Maps, Briefing Docs, Study Guides, Flashcards, Quizzes, Infographics, Slide Decks, Data Tables. Every output, explained and demonstrated.

**Go deeper.** Deep Research, customization, Interactive Audio Overviews, the 1M-token context window, multilingual notebooks, and the workflows that separate casual users from power users.

**Use it for your world.** Targeted chapters for students, researchers, knowledge workers, analysts, and content creators — each with realistic walkthroughs.

**Share and collaborate.** Public sharing, embedded outputs, and Plus-tier collaborative notebooks for teams, classes, and research groups.

**Stay safe and informed.** Honest coverage of citation drift, the 2026 voice-cloning lawsuit, what NotebookLM gets wrong, and the ethics of AI-assisted research.

**60+ ready-to-use prompt templates** organized by category — research, study, briefing, content creation, comparison, and analysis.

Whether you're opening NotebookLM for the first time or you've used Audio Overviews and want the rest of the toolkit, this is the only book you need.

Includes coverage of Gemini 3 integration, Cinematic Video Mode, Infographics, Slide Decks, Data Tables, the four-tier pricing restructure, and every 2026 feature.
